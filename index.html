How the sound gets down the line, and who gets to press skip.

The rig

One weak link

Right now the phone reaches the first speaker over Bluetooth, and that speaker feeds the SKAA transmitter by cable. Everything after that is rock solid. The Bluetooth hop is the fragile bit.

  • SoundBoks 4, Mix and Go have SKAA built in — they can join the cluster without a transmitter of their own.
  • One transmitter feeds up to four speakers, held within 40 millionths of a second of each other.

Range

45 metres, then relay

One transmitter covers about 45 metres. To go further, a second SKAA unit set to receiver mode picks up the signal, feeds a speaker by cable, and that speaker starts a fresh cluster — exactly what Peter already does.

Free coverage

Ride the sound bike in the middle of the line rather than the front. Same kit, same relay, roughly twice the parade covered.

Aerials

Get the signal over the heads

Conor is right, and the physics backs him. 2.4 GHz is absorbed by water, and a crowd is mostly water. A line of riders between two aerials is a wall.

The control question

Who gets to press skip

Spotify Connect only ever lists devices signed into your own account, so it's out unless everybody shares one login. Spotify Jam is the version built for separate accounts: the sound bike's phone plays, everyone else keeps their own Spotify and gets a remote.

Free accounts

A Free account can join in person and add songs, but can't host, can't join by link from a distance, and reportedly can't skip. On a moving parade every join is a distant one — so assume each admin needs their own Premium.

You don't need Tailscale

Jam runs through Spotify's servers over ordinary mobile data — no shared network at any point. A tailnet wouldn't help anyway: it can't carry the local-discovery traffic that would be the only reason to want one.

The one rule

Only one device makes a sound

This is what actually went wrong last time. In a Jam every phone streams its own copy — harmless, until two of them are driving speakers. Then there are two clocks, and they come apart.

Press play

Everybody together. This is the bit that gets tested, and it always looks fine.

Thirty seconds

The phone on a Bluetooth speaker is already a fraction of a beat late. Nothing compensates for it.

Two minutes

Most of a bar apart. Riders report gaps of three to seven seconds in the wild.

The fix

Not a network. Just keep the count at one: admins mute their own phones and plug into nothing.

Your phone is a remote,
not a speaker.

On the night

The whole checklist

Before you leave

The sound bike

  1. Phone wired straight into the SKAA transmitter. Volume ~80%, real volume on the transmitter.
  2. Download the playlist offline — a dead spot then can't stop the music.
  3. Aerial up on a mast, above head height.
  4. Do Not Disturb on. Mobile data on. Battery pack.
  5. Start the playlist, start the Jam, then Jam details → Guest settings → guest controls ON.
  6. Paste the Jam link into the group chat.
Two to five people

The admins

  1. Stay on your own Spotify. You need Premium.
  2. Tap the Jam link in the group chat — works over mobile data, from anywhere in the line.
  3. Your own volume to zero. Plug into nothing, pair with nothing.
  4. Skip, pause and queue from your own app.

Two fallbacks

If a Jam doesn't suit

One shared account

Bulletproof, but you log out of your own

Make one Bike Disco Premium account, put every phone on it, and use Spotify Connect. No links, no toggles, nothing to set up each time — and only one subscription in the whole system, so it doesn't matter what plan anyone is on. The cost: you're signed out of your own Spotify for the night, and whoever presses play without picking DISCO PHONE first steals the music.

A page with three big buttons

Easiest of all, needs an afternoon's work

Spotify's API can drive the sound bike's playback directly. Put SKIP, PAUSE and QUIETER on a web page and the admins need no Spotify account, no Premium, no app and no link — just a bookmark they can hit wearing gloves. Needs a small server to hold the token, so it can't be this site alone.
